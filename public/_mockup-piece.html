A six-prong platinum solitaire built around a 2.00ct round brilliant, cut to return light rather than to hold weight. The basket is kept deliberately narrow so the stone sits close to the hand.

Each Sovereign is set by a single bench jeweller from start to finish. The shank is 2.0mm, comfortable-fit, and can be sized within a half-size either way after purchase at no cost.
